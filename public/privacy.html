Privacy policy — RIOTSRADIOS

Last updated 6 November 2026. Applies to the Android app (com.mrhumildad.riotsradios) and to the web player at mrhumildad.github.io/riotsradios.

Short version: RIOTSRADIOS collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchase. The developer receives no personal data from the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, profile, email address or phone number is requested.
  • No analytics, telemetry, usage statistics or crash-reporting service is built in, and no third-party SDK of that kind is bundled.
  • No advertising identifier and no ads SDK: the app does not request the AD_ID permission.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location. The app does not request those permissions.
  • Your listening history, station choices and volume are not transmitted anywhere. Preferences may be kept locally on your device so the app reopens the way you left it.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Network connections the app makes

There are only two, and both are necessary for the app to work:

  • Radio streams. When you press play, the app connects directly to the broadcaster's own streaming server — a third party, not the developer. That server receives your IP address and the usual HTTP request information, exactly as it would from a web browser. What each broadcaster does with that is governed by its own privacy policy, which the developer does not control.
  • Typefaces. The interface loads its two fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), which receives your IP address as part of serving the files. No identifying information is added to that request.

The station catalog and TV-guide data shown in the app are shipped inside the app itself; they are not fetched from the developer at runtime.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • Internet — to play the stream you asked for.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — so audio keeps playing when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Notifications — for the playback notification that Android requires while a media service runs. Declining it does not stop playback.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Hosting of this page and the web player

The web player and this policy are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this page is updated and the date above changes with it. The URL of this page stays the same.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project repository.
